Our story of dignity, mercy, and excellence

Founded by community leaders in Lagos, Al Ihsan Relief and Empowerment blends humanitarian best practices with Islamic values to strengthen vulnerable families and cultivate global-minded Muslim youths.

Vision

A world where every vulnerable family thrives with dignity, self-reliance, and unwavering hope.

Mission

Deliver evidence-driven relief, empower livelihoods, and raise outstanding Muslim youths for global impact through ethical leadership and transparent governance.

Values

Ihsan (excellence), Adl (justice), Amanah (trust), Rahmah (compassion), and accountability to Allah and humanity.

Raising outstanding Muslim youths for global impact

Our youth development initiatives mentor future leaders in civic engagement, entrepreneurship, and faith-grounded service so they become catalysts for sustainable change across Africa.

Leadership Academies

Quarterly cohorts focused on governance, service design, and ethics-driven management.

Global Volunteer Corps

Structured placements with partner NGOs to build global humanitarian competencies.

Community Accountability

Every youth cohort serves in local masjid projects and reports outcomes publicly.
